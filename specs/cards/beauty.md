# BEAUTY — Gold · ✦ · Attention · cards 21–30

Territory energy: **Attention.** Some things should exist simply because they make being alive feel like being alive.

## 21/52 — THE GOLDEN HOUR

- **Type**: Adventure • Pursuit
- **Quest**:
  Learn today's sunset time.
  Arrive somewhere beautiful forty minutes before — a bridge, a rooftop, a field's edge, a west-facing window.
  Stay until the light is gone.
- **Proof of Life**: Keep one photograph of the last light.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **Stay for Blue** — you may not leave at sunset; the best color comes after.
- **Art direction**: a rooftop at the exact minute the sun drops, the whole city turning rose-gold, long shadows pooling like spilled ink.
- **Flavor**: "The best light arrives late and never apologizes."

## 22/52 — THE WINDOW AS A FRAME

- **Type**: Adventure • Creation
- **Quest**:
  Find the best window in your home or your street.
  Frame what it shows. Compose it deliberately: what is in, what is out, what is lit.
  Title it like a museum piece.
- **Proof of Life**: The photograph, printed or pinned, with a hand-written gallery label beneath it.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **Museum Label** — you must give it a title and a one-line provenance.
- **Art direction**: an ordinary apartment window floating alone in a gallery wall, the street beyond hung like a canvas, a small brass plaque beneath the sill.
- **Flavor**: "Beauty is not in things. It waits in the framing."

## 23/52 — FLOWERS FOR NO OCCASION — CANON

- **Type**: Adventure • Offering
- **Quest**:
  Buy flowers.
  There may be no birthday.
  No anniversary.
  No dinner party.
  No reason.
  Choose entirely by beauty.
- **Proof of Life**: Press one petal and preserve it with this card.
- **Special Ability**: **Useless Beauty** — you may not explain or justify the purchase.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Art direction**: an enormous, almost enchanted flower stall appearing unexpectedly on a gray city street.
- **Flavor**: *"Some things should exist simply because they make being alive feel like being alive."*

## 24/52 — THE SLOW LOOK

- **Type**: Adventure • Pursuit
- **Quest**:
  Choose one thing — a painting, a building's facade, a tree, a face in a photograph.
  Look at it for ten uninterrupted minutes.
- **Proof of Life**: Sketch three details you would have sworn were not there.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **Ten Minutes Is Forever** — no phone, no pacing; if your mind wanders, bring it back like a stray cat.
- **Art direction**: a figure standing motionless before one enormous painting in a vast gallery, the painting's colors slowly bleeding out into the room.
- **Flavor**: "Nothing reveals itself to a passing eye."

## 25/52 — THE FALLEN COLLECTION

- **Type**: Adventure • Creation
- **Quest**:
  Walk one street or shoreline slowly.
  Gather only what the ground offers freely — leaves, sea glass, tickets, feathers, one good stone.
  Assemble them on your table like a museum of the overlooked.
- **Proof of Life**: Photograph the collection as a whole; keep it in a dish.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **Only What Falls** — you may not pick anything still attached.
- **Art direction**: a windowsill museum of leaves, sea glass, and a yellowing ticket, each item resting on a scrap of hand-lettered label.
- **Flavor**: "The ground is a museum with no admission fee."

## 26/52 — THREE FACES OF LIGHT

- **Type**: Adventure • Discovery
- **Quest**:
  Photograph the same subject three times in one day — at dawn, at noon, at dusk.
  Keep all three.
  Notice which one is telling the truth.
- **Proof of Life**: The three photographs, kept together in order.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **No Choosing Sides** — all three stay.
- **Art direction**: a single tree shown in three panels — silver dawn, hard noon, molten dusk — the panels sharing one continuous horizon.
- **Flavor**: "Nothing is seen once."

## 27/52 — THE BEAUTY TAX

- **Type**: Adventure • Offering
- **Quest**:
  Take the ugliest errand on your list.
  Route it past one beautiful thing — a mural, a garden gate, a good window.
  Stop for five minutes. Pay yourself in beauty.
- **Proof of Life**: Photograph the beautiful stop and keep the errand's receipt together.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **No Skipping** — the detour is not optional; the errand is not done without it.
- **Art direction**: a gray government-office street where one impossible garden gate glows like a portal, its light spilling across the pavement toward the queue outside.
- **Flavor**: "Beauty does not ask to be on the way. It rewards whoever chooses the longer door."

## 28/52 — THE ARRANGEMENT

- **Type**: Adventure • Creation
- **Quest**:
  Compose a still life from five things you already own.
  Light it like a painting — one lamp, late hour.
  Leave it standing for a full day where you will pass it.
- **Proof of Life**: Photograph it; keep the photograph as the artifact.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **Nothing Bought** — every object must already be yours.
- **Art direction**: a midnight still life on a kitchen table, one lamp falling like a spotlight, ordinary objects arranged with the gravity of crown jewels.
- **Flavor**: "Beauty is a decision, not a purchase."

## 29/52 — THE COLOR HUNT

- **Type**: Adventure • Pursuit
- **Quest**:
  Choose one color.
  Walk your streets for an hour noticing only that color.
  Photograph your ten best finds.
- **Proof of Life**: The photographs, kept as one grid.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **One Thread** — once chosen, the color may not be changed.
- **Art direction**: a gray city street where one single color — a door, a coat, a ribbon — burns in full saturation while everything else fades to pencil sketch.
- **Flavor**: "Decide what you are hunting, and the world starts feeding you."

## 30/52 — VISIBLE MENDING

- **Type**: Adventure • Creation
- **Quest**:
  Mend something you were going to throw away — a sock, a book spine, a chair, a hole in a sweater.
  Make the repair beautiful on purpose.
  Do not hide it.
- **Proof of Life**: Photograph the repaired thing in use.
- **Reward**: Place your evidence in the Archive. This card is now **Lived**.
- **Special Ability**: **Gold in the Crack** — the repair must be visible, not disguised.
- **Art direction**: golden light across a wooden table, a needle mid-stitch, a ceramic bowl with a seam of gold glowing like a river through it.
- **Flavor**: "A mended thing carries two stories: the breaking and the returning."
