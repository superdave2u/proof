Rules for spec authors (Ralph):
- **CANON cards (marked CANON) are operator-authored and must never be edited.** They are the style bar; match their voice and concreteness.
- Search before creating — never assume a spec slot is missing or exists.
- No contradictions between files; SPEC.md wins ties.
- Card content is substantive and final — no TODOs, no lorem ipsum inside authored cards. Pending slots are work items, not content.
